# 3D Printable Battery Holder Guide

## File Information

**Filename:** `battery_hanger_-_led_sign_v4.stl`

**Purpose:** Secure mounting and hanging solution for LiFePO4 6V 6Ah batteries used in portable LED sign configurations.

---

## Print Settings

### Recommended Settings

**Material:**
- **Primary choice:** PLA (easiest, rigid)
- **Alternative:** PETG (more durable, heat resistant)
- **Not recommended:** TPU/flexible (won't hold battery securely)

**Print Quality:**
- **Layer height:** 0.2mm (standard)
  - Can use 0.15mm for smoother finish
  - Can use 0.3mm for faster print (acceptable)
- **Infill:** 20-30%
  - 20% minimum for adequate strength
  - 30% for heavier duty applications
- **Wall count:** 3-4 perimeters
- **Top/bottom layers:** 4-5 layers

**Print Speed:**
- Standard speed: 50-60 mm/s
- Perimeters: 40-50 mm/s (for better quality)
- First layer: 20-25 mm/s

**Supports:**
- **Check in slicer** - may or may not be needed
- If needed, use tree supports for easier removal
- Support density: 15-20%

**Bed Adhesion:**
- Brim: Recommended (5mm)
- Raft: Not necessary
- Glue stick or hairspray for better adhesion

## Post-Processing

### After Printing

1. **Remove from bed:**
   - Let cool completely (prevents warping)
   - Use scraper if needed

2. **Remove supports (if used):**
   - Use pliers or flush cutters
   - Clean up support marks with hobby knife

3. **Check fit:**
   - Test battery insertion before mounting
   - Battery should fit snugly but not too tight
   - Should be removable without excessive force

4. **Smooth edges (optional):**
   - Light sanding with 220-grit sandpaper
   - Focus on edges that might contact battery
   - Remove any stringing or blobs

5. **Test strength:**
   - Flex the mounting points gently
   - Ensure no weak spots or cracks
   - Re-print if structural issues found

## Installation Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Mount

1. Clean 3D print thoroughly
2. Remove any support material completely
3. Test-fit battery before final mounting
4. Check all cable routing paths

### Step 2: Attach to Sign/Surface

**Mounting options:**

**Option A: Screws (Permanent)**
- Locate mounting holes in print
- Use #6 or M3 screws
- Mount to back of LED sign frame
- Or mount to wall/surface

**Option B: Strong adhesive (Semi-permanent)**
- Use 3M VHB double-sided tape
- Or construction adhesive
- Clean both surfaces first
- Press firmly for 30 seconds

**Option C: Velcro (Removable)**
- Industrial strength Velcro strips
- One side on holder, one on sign
- Easy removal for transport
- May need to replace periodically

### Step 3: Route Cables

1. Insert battery into holder
2. Route power cables through cable management channels
3. Connect to XL4015 buck converter
4. Secure cables with zip ties or cable clips
5. Leave enough slack for battery removal

### Step 4: Test

1. Remove and re-insert battery several times
2. Ensure holder maintains secure grip
3. Check that cables don't get pinched
4. Verify mounting is stable
5. Test with sign hanging (if wall mount)

## Troubleshooting

### Battery doesn't fit / too tight

**Problem:** Battery won't insert or is stuck
**Solutions:**
- Scale print to 101-102% in slicer
- Sand interior surfaces lightly
- Check for support material remnants
- Verify battery dimensions match expected size

### Battery falls out / too loose

**Problem:** Battery not held securely
**Solutions:**
- Re-print at 100% scale (might have been scaled up)
- Add small foam padding strips
- Check print quality (may have warped)
- Add retention clip or strap

### Print warped or cracked

**Problem:** Structural issues with print
**Solutions:**
- Ensure proper bed adhesion (use brim/raft)
- Check bed leveling
- Increase wall count to 4
- Increase infill to 30%
- Print slower (better layer adhesion)
- Use enclosure (prevents cooling warping)

### Mounting holes don't align

**Problem:** Screw holes don't match your sign
**Solutions:**
- Use adhesive mounting instead
- Drill new holes if PLA
- Add mounting bracket adapter
- Print custom adapter plate

### Cables get pinched

**Problem:** Cable management isn't working
**Solutions:**
- Route cables differently
- Enlarge cable channels with knife/file
- Use thinner wires
- External cable clips instead

## Safety Considerations

### Before Using

✓ **Inspect print quality**
- No cracks or weak points
- All layers fused properly
- No delamination

✓ **Test weight capacity**
- Battery + holder = ~1.5 lbs
- Mounting must support this safely
- Use appropriate wall anchors if wall-mounting

✓ **Check heat resistance**
- PLA softens at ~60°C (140°F)
- Keep away from heat sources
- Don't use in direct sunlight for extended periods
- PETG is better for hot environments

✓ **Electrical safety**
- Ensure battery terminals don't short
- Route wires safely through channels
- No exposed metal touching print
- Secure all connections

⚠️ **Do NOT:**
- Drop the mounted battery
- Expose to extreme temperatures
- Use damaged/cracked prints
- Mount in unstable locations
- Exceed weight limits
